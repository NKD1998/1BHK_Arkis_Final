01 Overview

The next chapter has already begun

Versova has always been one of Mumbai’s most distinctive neighbourhoods, connected to the city, close to the coast and alive with its own unmistakable character. Now, the precinct around SVP Nagar is preparing for its next chapter.

A large scale cluster redevelopment has been approved for the wider SVP Nagar colony. Planned at a neighbourhood scale, it is expected to bring renewed homes, stronger civic infrastructure, better organised public spaces and a more contemporary urban environment.

Positioned along the edge of this evolving neighbourhood, ESTO Arkis gives homebuyers the opportunity to own a contemporary home in Versova today, while being part of an area preparing for tomorrow.
